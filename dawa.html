Scheduling

Set Once.
Remind Forever.

When logging a medication, patients set a start date, end date, and interval in hours. DawaTrack computes exactly how many doses per day and schedules SMS reminders at each dose time — automatically, for the entire prescription period.

The Formula
Doses per day = 24 ÷ Interval Hrs
SMS fires at: First Dose Time + every Interval Hrs

Both the patient and their nominated caregivers receive a personalised SMS at each dose time — no app needed on the caregiver's device.

SMS Reminders

Two messages.
One dose time.

Every time a dose is due, DawaTrack fires two distinct SMS messages: one personalised to the patient, and one to each nominated caregiver.

1
Patient gets their own SMS
With their medicine name, DawaTrack link, and signed with their own name — personal and direct.
2
Each caregiver gets a personalised message
Addressed to them by name — so they know exactly who to remind and what medicine it is.
3
No smartphone needed
Caregivers receive a plain SMS text — works on any basic phone, no internet or app required.
Pharmacy

directly to your pharmacy.

Through DawaTrack, pharmacies receive real-time medicine requests from patients. Pharmacists can review the request, communicate directly with the patient for clarifications, and the prescribing doctor can also provide notes or updates on the prescription — all in one place.
